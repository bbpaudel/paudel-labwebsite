Within just a few months of establishing our lab, P3SysBioLab achieved Green Lab Certification! This milestone reflects our commitment to sustainable research practices and our participation in the University of Virginia's [Green Labs Program](https://sustainability.virginia.edu/), which supports UVA's broader sustainability goals.
